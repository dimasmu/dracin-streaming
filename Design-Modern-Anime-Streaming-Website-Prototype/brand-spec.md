# DRA.CIN — Brand Specification

## Purpose
Platform streaming drama pendek China dengan penemuan konten berbasis **Genre** dan **Platform** asal. Navigasi hanya Home, Genre, Platform, dan Schedule.

## Visual system
Near-black navy foundation (`--bg`), elevated navy cards (`--surface`), electric indigo as the sole action accent (`--accent`), and cyan only for language/status cues (`--cyan`). Typography pairs Barlow Condensed display with Manrope body.

## Content card rule
Normal drama cards are symmetrical 1:1 squares. Each card carries title, current episode, source platform, and subtitle language. Hover/focus reveals a restrained tooltip with a brief synopsis and supporting metadata.

## Language
Header toggle supports English (`EN`) and Indonesia (`ID`) subtitle modes; initial state is English.
