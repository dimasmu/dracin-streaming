1. Information We Collect

DRA.CIN is a visual prototype that functions as a metadata directory. In its current phase it does not actively collect personal data from its users.

Stored locally (in your browser only):

  • Subtitle preference (EN or ID) and theme preference, stored via localStorage. This data is never sent to a server.

Potentially received indirectly:

  • Details submitted through the contact form (name, email, subject and message) — used only to respond to you.

Development note: sections that depend on actual business practices are placeholders and require legal review before production use.

2. How We Use Data

Data we receive is used for:

  • Subtitle preference — stored locally and used to tailor metadata display.
  • Contact messages — used exclusively to respond to questions, bug reports or content requests.

DRA.CIN does not sell, trade or share your personal data for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Subtitle and theme preferences are stored in localStorage on your own browser. DRA.CIN has no access to your localStorage — the data stays on your device and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

Metadata submitted through the contact form would, in production, be stored only as long as needed.

4. Third-Party Sharing

DRA.CIN does not share your personal data with third parties, except:

  • Legal compliance — if required by applicable law or legal process.
  • Analytics placeholders — this prototype has not integrated third-party analytics that actively send data.

Note: when you click a link to a third-party streaming platform (for example DramaBox or ReelShort), that platform’s own privacy policy applies separately.

5. Your Rights

As a user you may:

  • Know what data we collect about you (if any).
  • Request deletion of personal data you have submitted.
  • Manage or delete the preferences stored in your browser.

To exercise these rights, use the Contact page.

6. DMCA Policy

DRA.CIN respects intellectual property rights. See the DMCA policy for removal requests.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated as the platform develops. Changes are reflected in the “Last updated” date at the top of this document. Continued use after changes means you accept the revised policy.

8. Contact

For questions about this policy, use the Contact page.
