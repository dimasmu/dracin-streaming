1. Scope of Policy

This policy applies to all content identified on the platform, including drama titles, synopses, artwork and other metadata. DRA.CIN is a metadata directory — it does not host or play video files.

If you are a content owner, or an authorised representative, and believe your copyrighted material is displayed on DRA.CIN without permission, the mechanism below lets you request removal.

Note: Removal requests apply only to metadata shown on DRA.CIN, not to content on third-party streaming platforms where dramas are hosted.

2. Infringement Notice

To request removal, you (or your authorised representative) must send a written notice that meets the DMCA requirements to our designated copyright agent.

Warning: Inaccuracy in a notice can result in liability for damages. If you are unsure whether material is infringing, consult a lawyer first.

2.1 Required Information

  • A physical or electronic signature of the rights holder or an authorised agent.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe is infringed, including title, year and platform.
  • The location of the infringement — the specific link or metadata section.
  • Contact information — full name, address, phone number and email.
  • A good-faith statement that the use is not authorised by the owner, their agent, or the law.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that the information is accurate and you are authorised to act for the rights owner.

2.2 Filing Steps

1

Prepare a Written Notice

Gather all the information listed in the checklist above before proceeding.

2

Send it to Our Agent

Send the complete notice to the email address listed at the bottom of this page.

3

Wait for Review

Our team will review the notice and take appropriate action, which may include removal or disabling access.

4

Confirmation

We will send confirmation to the email address provided in the notice once action is taken.

3. Counter-Notification

If you believe content was removed or disabled in error, you may submit a counter-notification. It must include information similar to the original notice and a statement under penalty of perjury that the removal was a mistake. We will process it in line with DMCA requirements.

4. Repeat Infringers

DRA.CIN takes firm action against repeat infringement. Where a user is found to infringe repeatedly, their access may be suspended or terminated after two or more valid notices.

5. Contact

To file a DMCA request, use the Contact page or email us directly.
